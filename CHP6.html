The Last Consequence: Birth of the Darkpoint

Chapter 6: Unseen Potential

I woke up tiredly, initially thinking I had just dreamt all of it—which wasn’t true at all. I got off my bed, rubbing my eyes with my hand, and yawned as I walked to the door. I opened it and began strolling around the base. In the kitchen, I searched for some food, but there was only nutritious food available—not a single chocolate bar or anything even slightly unhealthy. I didn’t care and feasted until I wasn’t hungry anymore. Having slept well and eaten as healthily as possible, I felt strong again.

I felt like exploring, which I did until I was confronted by James. He stood there slurping coffee, dressed in regular clothes rather than his usual suit or undercover outfit. In a relaxing tone, he said to me, "Buddy, you’ve been sleeping way too long." He looked down at his modern watch, nodded slightly, and told me it was time to continue training.

I was disappointed, but having no other choice, I followed him to the gym. How could he afford to have all of this in one place, and had he always lived here by himself? James explained that he had to make me a near-perfect being, preparing me both physically and mentally. Having an ability isn’t a complete gift—if you can’t control it, you could easily become a danger to everyone. It was James’s responsibility to lead me onto the correct path: to become a protector, not a threat to humanity.

My first challenge was to complete 10 push-ups, which didn’t seem difficult to me. I lowered myself to the ground, extending my legs back and pushing with both arms. I pushed myself up and down until he placed a 50-pound kettlebell on my back. It was far too heavy. I wasn’t weak, but working out had never been my thing; while I wasn't super skinny, I had only ever cared about studying and making money. This whole situation was never planned. I couldn't move even a fraction of an inch, feeling almost paralyzed under the weight.

James lifted the kettlebell off me and said in a strict tone, "You’re far from being ready."

Pull-ups came up next. I placed my hands on the iron bar, ensuring a solid grip. Looking to my right, I saw James standing with crossed arms, giving me a stern look. I gave it everything I had just to do a single pull-up, only to collapse to the ground right after. These were only the initial exercises, and I kept going, doing far more than I thought I ever could.

Finally came the last exercise. The intensity made my face steam red from sheer exhaustion. I wasn’t angry—I was completely drained. I was swaying around, my heart beating rapidly, and my head spinning so badly I felt dizzy as hell. At the same time, I was nauseous, feeling as if I was about to throw up all over the gym floor.

While my vision was blurry, James disappeared. I stumbled toward the bathroom, struggling to walk and keep my balance. I leaned my arm against the wall, shuffling forward step by step. The only thing that helped was forcing myself to inhale and exhale slowly.

I shoved open the door and forcefully drank water straight out of the bathroom sink. The cool water helped me feel slightly better, but then I tasted blood and felt a sharp pain in my throat. I lowered my head and began coughing blood, spitting into the sink and splattering the mirror. Everything hurt. I started shaking violently and gripped the edges of the sink. Raising my head, I saw myself, the sink, and the mirror covered in blood. I smiled faintly before my eyes rolled upward. My vision faded into darkness, and I went unconscious, losing my grip and crash-landing onto the floor.

Starting a routine this intense was never going to be easy; I was constantly exhausted no matter what. Over the next few months, however, I learned to control my powers and strengthen my weaknesses.

I had to perform these workouts every third day of the week, with one day allocated for a long break. The weekly schedule was divided into two days of physical training, two days of mental training, two days of ability training, and the final day was dedicated to a new challenge to unlock a new skill. I understood physical training, but the other subjects weren't easy either. I had to read countless books, master general academic subjects, and even learn how to read people’s facial expressions to predict their next moves. It was challenging, but compared to the physical toll, it was the easiest part for me because I am naturally intelligent.

Then there was ability training, where I had to lift, crush, or split various objects. We started with a wooden block weighing 5 kilograms—the heaviest object I had ever attempted to lift, considering my previous record with my schoolbag was only half that weight.

The weekly final challenges were by far the hardest. The first one involved running through the snow, which originally took place on the second day before being moved to Sunday. I had to sprint through freezing conditions and adjust my body until I managed to return to the base in just 10 minutes. Since I never knew where I would be dropped off, I had to memorize every square mile of the surrounding location. Mastering this single challenge took months.

The only reason my body could adapt that quickly was because of my particle manipulation. It didn't just grant me the power to move external particles—it also allowed me to manipulate my own body's particles to recover exponentially faster as I grew stronger physically and mentally.

Another difficult task required me to attack James directly. I had to learn combat techniques and adapt to his blinding pace. Naturally, I never won a single match—I was nowhere near as strong as he was.

Yet after six months of relentless conditioning, the tasks that once seemed impossible became a piece of cake. It was finally time for a more difficult challenge—something that wouldn't be quite so easy.
