The Last Consequence: Birth of the Dark Spiral

Chapter 6: Unseen Potential

I tiredly woke up, thinking he had just dreamt which wasn’t true at all. I got off my bed rubbing my eyes with my hand, yawning while I walked to the door. I opened the door and strolling around the base. In the kitchen I searched for some food but there was only nutritious food and not a single chocolate bar or anything slightly unhealthy. I didn’t care and feasted till I wasn’t hungry anymore. I had slept well and feasted as healthy as possible, I felt strong again. I felt like exploring which I did till I was confronted by James. He stood there slurping coffee dressed regularly not like most times in which he would either wear his suit or his undercover costume. With a relaxing tone he said to me: Buddy you have been sleeping way too long. He looked down on his modern watch, nodded slightly telling me it was time to continue the training. I was disappointed but had no other choice then I just followed him to the gym. The fact that he all had that in just one place, how could he afford that and did he always live here by himself. James explained he had to make a near to perfect being, therefore preparing me physically and mentally, having an ability isn’t a complete gift, if you can’t control it then you could just be danger to everyone. It was James’s responsibility to lead me to correct path to become a protector not a threat to humanity.

First Challenge I had to complete 10 push ups which didn’t seem difficult to me. I lowered myself to the ground and stretching my legs to back and pushing both arms to the ground. I pushed myself up and down till he put a 50-pound kettlebell on me. It was way to heavy; I wasn’t weak but working out wasn’t my thing really, I wasn’t that skinny but I only cared about learning and making money, this whole thing was never planned. In this situation I couldn’t even a bit, almost like being paralyzed. James lifted the kettlebell of me and said in a strict: You’re far from being ready’’. Pull ups came up next, I laid my hands on the iron bar, making sure I had a good grip on the bar. Looked to right, seeing James standing there with crossed arms, he was giving me a strict look. I gave it all just to do only pull up and to collapse to the ground. These were only exercises; I kept going doing way more than I had thought I would do.

Then finally the last exercise, it all made me steaming red out of exhaustion, I wasn’t angry, I was just complete hungover. I was swaying around, heart beating rapidly, eyes spinning making me dizzy as hell. At the same time, I was nauseous felling like I was about throw up all over the gym. While my vision was blurry James disappeared, I head to the bathroom, struggling to walk and keep balance. I leaned my arm against the wall and going forward while keeping my hand on the wall. The only thing that helped was to inhale and exhale slowly. I shoved open the door, drinking water forcefully out the bathroom sink. The fresh water helped me getting better but then I tasted blood, I felt pain in my throat. I lowered my head down, starting coughing blood, spitting into the sink whilst covering even slightly the mirror. Everything hurt, I started shaking, I gripped the outer part of the sink. I raised my head seeing myself, the sink and the mirror covered in blood, I smiled then my eyes glanced upwards. My vision faded into darkness, I went unconscious, losing gripping and falling to crash land to the floor.

Yeah, it wasn’t easy, quickly starting a training this hard, I was always exhausted no matter what. In the next months I learned to control my powers and strengthen my weaknesses. I had to perform these exercises every third day in the week, there was a day in which I could take a long break. The week was divided into 2 days physical training, 2 days mental training, 2 days ability training and the last day was always a new challenge to achieve a new skill. I now knew what physical training was but the other things weren’t easy either. I had to read lots of books, learn every general topic or even learnt how to read people’s expressions and predict their next moves, it was a challenge but compared to the other training it was the easiest because I am generally intelligent.

And then there’s the ability training in which I had to lift, crush or split something starting with wood block which weighed 5 Kilograms, it was the heaviest I had ever attempted to lift, the schoolbag which was my previous record was only half of that. The first ever challenge or the hardest of them was the weekly final challenge first was of the run through the snow which for some reason was on the second day, the weekly final was then moved to Sunday meaning I had to run through the snow, adjusting my body till I managed to get back to base in just 10 Minutes, I never knew where I would start therefore had to memorize every location, this final took me months. The only reason why my body even could adjust that quickly was because of my ability, it not only gave me the powers to manipulate my own particles to move them to a certain point but also to recover even quicker the stronger I mentally and physically got. Another quite difficult was that I had to sometimes attempt to attack James, I had to learn fighting techniques and adjust to his tempo, I of course never won because I’m far away from even being as strong as him.

After all of this, the challenges which were immensely impossible for me, were now after 6 months a piece of cake. The time had come for more difficult challenges something more demanding. This time he didn’t just strand me anywhere but he accompanied to a huge snowy mountain. I was a few thousand meters tall, taking a while to get up to it. When we arrived at the highest point, I lowered my head down out of exhaustion whilst seeing the bottom. It was quite high up and making me nervous. I didn’t really have a fear of height though this was a far from the height I’m used to. I looked back to James attempting to ask him what to do. He stretched himself and then said, "Save yourself Kid" like a joke. I thought I just had to run down in a certain time. I smirked and said, "this won't be difficult."

He pushed me off the mountain and started speeding towards the bottom. I was helplessly falling and attempting to grip the mountains ridges. They kept breaking and I started screaming: "WHAT THE FUCK IS THIS, YOU ARE REALLY TRYING TO KILL ME AGAIN!" I didn’t see him run down therefore I thought I just had to save myself without his help. I faced down, closed my eyes, and concentrated my particles. I was about to hit the ground and I tried lifting myself. It didn’t work. I crossed my arms and hoped I wouldn’t hit any of the spikes that were peeking through the snow. Will he save me or is this the last time he saves me? I squeezed my eyes out of fear. Death always comes closer than you would ever think…
