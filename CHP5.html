The Last Consequence: Birth of the Dark Spiral

Chapter 5: The Spiral Made Out of Dark Materia

There is a hypothesis that the Big Bang created our universe, but the question remains: what created the Big Bang, what created the multiverse, or even beyond that? What or who created all of it? There is no real answer, but what if it was all created by itself? If it created itself, that means there was no true beginning, or perhaps it occurred before time even existed. Nothing can be proven, but one thing is clear: darkness has always been a part of the multiverse, and somehow, it formed a spiral—the spiral of dark Materia.

Assembled from manipulated matter the size of entire galaxies combined, far away from Earth, the spiral's color was darker than anything else in existence. Bright edges formed around the dark Materia, though they could not make the spiral any brighter. At its core was an entity in constant motion, shifting its behavior with every passing second. As time went on, it expanded and began to split apart; something had been imprisoned or created over billions of years before humanity ever existed.

The core breached, falling and shattering into trillions of pieces. Through this process, a single life form was born from the dark matter—the birth of one of the most powerful creatures in existence: the Crexemtm, or its full Latin name, Creatura exitii et mortis totius materiae, meaning the Creature of Destruction and Death of All Matter.

The slimy mass inside the core formed a small creature compared to the immense core itself, standing only slightly taller than an average human. The creature lacked defined limbs and drifted through space without physical movement until it approached one of the earliest developed worlds: Earth 121. The creature breached the atmosphere and landed safely on the surface.

The planet flourished with nature—plants of vibrant orange and blue—alongside architecture as modern as our own. Its inhabitants did not quite resemble humans, bearing a few distinct differences: they possessed four sharp eyes with orange pupils, stood approximately seven feet tall, and had a far more advanced physiology.

The Crexemtm glided across the soil toward the nearest city, absorbing small amounts of earth as it moved and leaving behind a dark, stained trail. Upon reaching the edge of town, it halted. A young inhabitant noticed the creature and, driven by curiosity rather than fear, leaned down and extended a hand toward it.

Drawn to the scent and flesh, the creature extended small tentacles toward the hand. As soon as they touched, the creature latched onto the child, slowly melting his hand. Terrified, the youngling struggled to pull his arm free from the blob, but the entity pulled him closer. His screams echoed across the town, drawing immediate attention.

An elder inhabitant rushed forward and forcefully pulled the boy away, tearing off parts of his arm in the process. Half of the youngling's arm had already been dissolved, leaving him trembling in terror. As the creature digested the flesh, it began to bubble.

Other inhabitants formed a protective circle around the creature while carrying the injured child away. The elder seized a spear and impaled the entity with full force. It stopped bubbling, raised its mass, and began to reform—shapeshifting right before their eyes.

It replicated the form of the elder standing before it and grabbed the spear's shaft. The creature broke and absorbed the spear effortlessly. The elder stepped back in sheer shock and terror. The entity remained completely unharmed, staring at the gathered crowd as one inhabitant shouted with pride, "Stay away, creature, or we will defeat you by any means!"

It did not respond, standing motionless while swaying its head, before taking a single step toward the elder. The elder swung a powerful fist at the creature's face; his arm passed right through its head, becoming trapped halfway inside the thick mass. When he violently tore his arm free, it was severely wounded and bleeding heavily. The creature tilted its hand and severed the elder's head in an instant.

The head hit the soil as blood pooled around the feet of the onlookers. Though paralyzed with fear, the remaining inhabitants launched a desperate attack. They failed miserably.

The creature slaughtered and absorbed every single inhabitant until none remained. Over millions of years, it systematically destroyed the entire planet in the same manner. A world that had once lived in peace and harmony—devoid of war and hatred—was completely erased. The planet itself fractured, falling apart into uninhabitable ruins.

Standing upon one of the surviving planetary fragments, the creature raised its newly acquired arms into the void, transferring its absorbed energy back to the spiral of dark Materia. The creature was merely an agent of the spiral, driven to consume all matter and energy to feed its growth.

This was the story of how evil was born, as told by James—or as he calls himself, "Crimson Riot." I now understood how it all began, and I knew I had to stop it. I didn't think of myself as the last hope, but I swore to keep fighting regardless.

The quote I kept repeating to myself until the very end: "The day I die, evil falls."
