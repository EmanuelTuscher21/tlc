The Last Consequence: Birth of the Dark Spiral

Chapter 4: Wintertime

Greenland—an autonomous region belonging to Denmark, almost as big as Algeria in size, but with a population comparable to the Caribbean microstate of Dominica. A cold, perfect place to hide secrets, largely because most of its area is covered in snow. We had fled from the cops to a base never to be found—or so we thought.

After the huge door closed, we both entered exhausted, even though James didn’t look even a bit out of power. He took off his mask and activated the elevator. As we slowly lowered to the main area of the base, I looked at him, feeling fear but also a strange sense of relief. No school, no stress—even though I never had a problem in school and was a straight-A student, it felt great to have freedom. What was I going to be anyway, a retail worker?

Then he turned to me and asked enthusiastically, "Wasn’t that fun? Running at high speeds above the Atlantic Ocean!"

I couldn’t believe it. I was curious and had to ask how fast he actually ran. He thought for a second, touching his chin, and then said proudly, "At least 2,000 miles per hour."

My eyes widened in shock, feeling like this couldn’t be real. He looked at me and told me that what was next would shock me even more.

The elevator doors opened to reveal modern furniture and glowing lights. The design was incredible. I yawned, realizing how badly I needed sleep. He showed me where everything was—the living room, bedroom, and the rest. I even had my own bedroom. The futuristic look amazed me. I touched the shining iron frame of the bed and slumped onto it, pulling the warm blanket over myself.

James looked inside and said, "Tomorrow you’re going to call me Crimson—only Crimson—because we’re having our first physical training. I’m serious!"

My eyes twitched, and my vision faded into darkness.

The next morning, I woke up feeling a freezing breeze around me. I opened my eyes to see the light blue sky. I was still wearing my school uniform, and my body was cooling down rapidly. I stood up and raised my arm to shield myself. Looking around, all I saw were trees and snow, remembering the words James had said to me.

He wanted to train me, but why put me in a life-or-death situation right away? I sprinted straight ahead, hoping to find something leading back to the base.

The longer I stayed in the cold, the slower I got. My hands went numb even though I kept rubbing them together. I started swaying from side to side, unable to think clearly through the sheer anger I felt. An hour went by, and my body was failing me, but I had finally found my way out of the forest.

The icy wind grew stronger and stronger—so harsh that I couldn’t see clearly. My legs hurt, and walking was my only option. It didn’t take long until I couldn’t walk anymore and kneeled on the snowy ground, breathing heavily. My heart slowed down. I raised my head upwards and screamed in agony as loud as I could.

No one heard me. I started crawling, losing hope with every passing second. The only choice I had left was to somehow use my particle manipulation.

It was extremely difficult to concentrate while lying flat on the ground with a pounding headache I had never experienced before. It took ten whole minutes just to reach the point where I could perceive the particles again. I forced the particles to vibrate against my body, generating heat to keep me warm. It was exhausting; I almost thought it was impossible.

Finally, it worked. My body began heating up again, and I stood up. One step, then another—I kept walking faster.

Another hour later, I spotted the mountain where the base was hidden. I rushed forward even as exhaustion threatened to overwhelm me; I couldn’t maintain the particle vibrations forever. I was reaching my absolute limit, but I refused to give up. 100 more steps... 50... 25... 10... and there it was.

I frantically dug through the snow in frustration, telling myself that if I saw that guy, I was going to smack the fuck out of him. I kept digging as the particle vibrations faded and the cold returned. Suddenly, I found the entrance—the door was slightly ajar, forming a narrow gap. I squeezed through, and the door sealed shut behind me.

The elevator descended slowly. I felt rough, exhausted, and furious. I stood there menacingly, breathing loudly and waiting for the doors to open.

The doors slid open, and I rushed toward James, screaming in pure rage, "Kidnapping me and then pulling this shit? What the fuck do you expect from me? I’m not Superman!"

James was peacefully slurping his coffee. He stood up from his chair, and I swung right at his head. He didn’t flinch or defend himself. Right after taking the hit, he simply cracked his neck and said that the punch wasn’t bad, but not enough.

I was still furious and tried to hit him harder. He instantly deflected my punch and slammed me to the ground with immense force. I lay there completely unconscious, with no idea what would happen next. Luck certainly wasn’t on my side today... maybe tomorrow would be better.
