The Last Consequence: Birth of the Dark Spiral

Chapter 1: What am I or What can I do?

Another boring day or so I thought. Instead of being sunny like yesterday, the sun was nowhere to be seen and the horizon only showed a dark gray mist softening all of the edges of trees and buildings. I stood up but my legs kept shaking, my eyes constantly blinked. I felt really nauseous, just taking a step felt like hell.

I I took one step then another and, in an instant, I felt such terrible pain, I fell. Now I just laid there on the ground helplessly. I raised my head then my upper body and at last my lower body. I legs were still shaking though I just fought through it and did my daily routine which is eating, preparing for school and making sure the house is fully secured like always.

After that I felt relieved and I stopped blinking constantly. Outside I calmly walked towards school whilst my body leaned left and right. My vision was blurry, I approached the road and I was about to step on the crosswalk, the l Traffic light was still bright red. Suddenly whilst my body leaned forward, a car came racing towards me.

I was about to be run over by a car but then my body froze halfway in place and my vision became clear again. I reacted instantly and with all my strength left in me pushed myself backwards. It worked, the car drove by and I had just survived something which could have been a tragic ending for me. I was shock, it felt like if had been lifted by an angel or something.

It was really strange also because I slightly saw an object in my mind which I had seen before, before I felt asleep. I couldn’t remember and just waited for the lights to finally turn green
