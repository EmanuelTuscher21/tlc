The Last Consequence: Birth of the Dark Spiral

Chapter 1: A Boring Start

The weather is in a good mood that day as sunlight shines through the small gap of my curtain and entered my room. Realizing that it's already morning, I forced myself to get up from my bed and start preparing myself for this boring day. Greetings, my name is Elias Crowe. I am a 16-year-old teenage boy who lives in Birmingham, England.

I have a brother named Liam that lives in the same small apartment as me. After getting dressed, I walk to my brother's room to wake him up, but to my surprise he is not there—only 20 pounds on his bed that he probably left for me. "At least it's still enough for some food," I said to myself, before putting the cash into my pocket and heading downstairs to eat breakfast.

In the kitchen I made myself a bowl of cereal which I ate in an instant. It wasn't a lot, but at least enough to keep full for now. After finishing, I prepared myself to go to school. On my way to school, I realized that the temperature had dropped drastically; luckily, I put my coat on earlier. I sighed, forcing myself to keep walking.

My brother and I didn't have much money, therefore I always had to walk a few miles to school every day. After walking for so long, I finally arrived at my school. I opened the double doors in front of me; I gasped out of exhaustion. My legs were getting sore, but I didn't give it a second thought and continued walking through the hallway to my class.

I walk to my classroom, wanting to take a seat and give myself some peace, but it turned out someone was already waiting for me. A banana peel was thrown right onto my face by Brian, one of my bullies. "Go on, throw it away little smartass," he said with arrogance, his friends immediately bursting out laughing at me.

I stared at them with disgust before I wiped the banana peel from my face and threw it into the nearest trash can. Right after that joke, they just walked past me with no guilt whatsoever, and Brian whispered, "See you later, Twat." Long story short, the day at my school passed quickly and I managed to endure all of the lessons while walking down the hall of bullies who screamed "peel face" at me.

I just completely ignored them, not giving a damn about it; I was just thinking they were not worth my precious time. After a long walk home, I finally arrived. I took off my jacket, put down my backpack, and took a seat in my chair before starting my homework. But as time passed, my vision started to blur.

For a moment I thought I was just exhausted, so I laid my head on the table, giving myself a moment to rest. But as I drifted off to sleep, I saw something—something that I had never seen. In my dream, I saw a bright green sphere in my mind.

The sphere multiplied itself into 2, then 4, 8, 16, and so on. Then the spheres collided into each other. The collision caused a shockwave which was enough to wake me up from my sleep. As soon as I woke up, I ran up to my room, not even caring about my homework or the cup of coffee that shattered on the floor.

It wasn’t a dream—I had just crushed a coffee cup without touching it. I entered my room and quickly tucked myself in, trying to forget what had just happened earlier, and I just fell asleep.

Hours later, my brother Liam finally arrived home. When he was about to go upstairs, he found the cup of coffee that shattered on the floor. He cleaned it without a second thought before finally going upstairs. Before entering his own room, he took a peek inside my bedroom.

Realizing that I'd already drifted into a deep sleep, he approached me, grabbed some money out of his wallet, and put it on my nightstand. After saying goodnight to me, he walked into his own room and prepared to end the day.

As the sun finally dipped below the horizon, the moon rose high into the sky. Midnight passed by peacefully. The day which would change my life forever was near...
